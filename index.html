Who is Ledger Fix Lab for?

New bookkeepers and students before their first client, and owners who do their own books and want to check their work.

Are the client files real?

No. Each file is a practice business written for the trainer, so no one's real books are shared. The mistakes in them are the common real ones.

Do I need QuickBooks or Xero?

No. It runs in the browser and teaches the cleanup itself, which is the same in any accounting software.

How is a file graded?

On the books you end with: nothing missing or extra, amounts matching the statement, cleared status, and categories. Any correct fix passes.

What is a balanced but wrong file?

The bank and books agree to the cent, but a line sits in the wrong category, so profit and taxes come out wrong.
