Why learn this now

Software enters the lines. Someone still has to check them

  1. Automation makes these mistakes

    A bank feed that reconnects can import the same lines again and put a duplicate entry in the books. An auto-category can guess that a transfer from savings is a sale.

    Intuit: fixing duplicate bank-feed transactions

  2. You answer for the result

    The IRS holds the taxpayer responsible for everything on the return, whoever or whatever prepared it. Someone has to check the books before they are signed.

    IRS Topic 254

  3. It is a tested skill

    Bank reconciliation and correcting errors are part of the Certified Bookkeeper exam of the American Institute of Professional Bookkeepers.

    AIPB: the Certified Bookkeeper designation

Questions

Before you start

Who is Ledger Fix Lab for?

New bookkeepers and students before a first client. Owners who keep their own books use it to check their habits.

Won't AI do the bookkeeping anyway?

Software already enters most lines, and it makes exactly the mistakes practiced here: doubled imports, wrong categories, owner money booked as sales. You can ask an AI to check, but you are the one who answers for the books, so you need to know what right looks like. This trains that.

I have never kept books. Can I start?

Yes. "Start from zero" teaches the five ideas you need in about two minutes, and every bookkeeping word can be tapped for a plain explanation.

Can I check my real books?

Yes. Put your bank statement and your books side by side as two CSV files, and the same matching finds lines entered twice, amounts that differ, bank lines nobody recorded and transfers filed as income. The files are read in your browser and are not uploaded. Sample files are included.

Do I get a certificate?

Not a certificate. You get a practice record: one page listing what you solved, how much of it without hints, and whether your practice is current (a file solved in the last 30 days). You can print it. Ledger Fix Lab is not an accredited body, and the record says so.

Are the client files real?

No. Each file is a practice business written for the trainer, so no one's real books are shared. The mistakes are the common real ones.

Do I need QuickBooks or Xero?

No. It runs in the browser. The cleanup steps are the same in any accounting software.

How is a file graded?

Four checks on the books you end with: each transaction recorded exactly once, amounts, cleared items, categories. Any correct fix passes.

What does balanced but wrong mean?

The bank and the books agree to the cent, but a line sits in the wrong category. Profit and tax come out wrong.
